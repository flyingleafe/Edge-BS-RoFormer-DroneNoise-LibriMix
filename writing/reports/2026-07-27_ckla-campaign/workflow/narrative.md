# Narrative — CKLA: a Kalman-structured temporal head, its mechanistic diagnosis, and the augmentation regime that feeds it
kind: report
audience: supervisor-level reader who has the 2026-07-24 VK-parity status report (knows the 2.481 floor, the VK bars, the G-series lever ledger; has NOT seen the CKLA work)
through-line: The VK-parity campaign stalled at a 3.4× structural gap with cheap levers exhausted. We replaced the transformer temporal head with a complex Kalman linear attention (CKLA) head, then — instead of blind iteration — instrumented the trained model and measured why it wins where it wins and loses where it loses (accumulator degeneration; amplitude anchoring shared by every architecture). Each measured pathology became a one-knob intervention, and both landed: the Kalman head now beats the matched transformer by 30% on full-envelope validation, with the cruise-pool verdict and the matched transformer+freqscale control in flight.
